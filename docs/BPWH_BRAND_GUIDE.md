# BP Water Heaters Brand Guide

This guide translates the supplied `Print 5.pdf` badge into a consistent digital brand system for the public site, customer portal, admin surfaces, emails, invoices, and future mobile app.

## Logo System

The official logo is the BP monogram inside the circle. Use `bpwh-logo.svg` for nav bars, favicons, app icons, login screens, customer portal headers, email headers, invoice headers, and compact UI.

The full badge from `bpwh-badge.svg` remains a secondary field badge. Use it only when the full sign has room to breathe: print, vehicle graphics, uniforms, large splash screens, signage, and social cover art.

Do:

- Use the BP circle mark as the primary logo.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Use live text for the phone number, service area, license number, and calls to action.
- Use the full badge only in large-format brand moments.

Do not:

- Treat the full badge as the logo.
- Put the full badge in nav bars, buttons, portal headers, or favicons.
- Stretch, crop, rotate, shadow, or recolor the logo.
- Use "BPWH" in customer-facing copy unless space absolutely requires it.

## Color

`#040C20` is the canonical digital navy. The PDF source uses a very close print navy, approximately `#06122C`; use the digital value in code so web, app, and ERP surfaces stay consistent.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#040C20` | Primary brand color, text, buttons, dark surfaces |
| Deep Blue | `#071A33` | Dark gradients and elevated dark panels |
| Soft Ink | `#1A2944` | Secondary text |
| Paper | `#F4F7FB` | Page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reverse text and cards |
| Steel Blue | `#44627F` | Quiet secondary accents |
| Warm Accent | `#C46F32` | Sparse emphasis only |
| Warm Highlight | `#F4C08E` | Small labels on dark backgrounds |

## Typography

Keep the current pairing:

- Fraunces: public-site display headlines and occasional brand moments.
- Archivo: body text, forms, portal panels, buttons, labels, status chips, admin UI, and transactional content.

The portal should use mostly Archivo. It should feel secure, clear, and operational, not like a second landing page.

## Voice

BP Water Heaters should sound practical, direct, and reassuring.

Use concrete copy: "Book a one-hour estimate," "Pay invoice," "Messages stay with this job," and "No online slots are open right now."

Avoid internal terms like "record" when "service portal," "job details," or "service history" is clearer to a customer.

## Interface Style

Use sturdy, squared surfaces with modest radius, crisp dividers, clear focus rings, and tabular numbers. Prefer spacing, hierarchy, and status chips over heavy decoration.

Icons should be functional, one-color, and simple. Use them only when they clarify an action or status, such as calendar, receipt, message, service, payment, and file.

## Portal Application

The customer portal should feel like a secure service workspace. Lead with what matters:

- What is next.
- What is owed.
- How to contact BP Water Heaters.

Use the BP circle mark in the header, clear status chips for bookings/invoices/messages, navy payment actions, and direct chat language. Do not use the full badge inside the portal UI.
