# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

This private repository supports BP Water Heaters business systems. Everyone
working in it is expected to be professional, direct, respectful, and careful
with customer and company information.

Good participation includes:

- Using clear and respectful language
- Keeping feedback focused on the work
- Protecting private customer, employee, vendor, and payment information
- Asking for clarification when a change could affect production operations
- Leaving the repository, issue tracker, and pull requests useful for the next
  person who has to reason about the system

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Sharing credentials, tokens, payment data, or private customer details
- Using issues or pull requests for unrelated personal, political, or abusive
  commentary
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional business setting

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported to the BP Water Heaters project maintainers through
the private repository or internal operations channel. Maintainers may edit,
close, or remove issues, pull requests, comments, or access that do not meet
this standard.
